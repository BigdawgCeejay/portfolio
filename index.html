About

I'm a full stack engineer who enjoys owning a feature end to end: shaping the user experience, building the server logic, and designing the data behind it. Through the i.c.stars program, I've been building real-world projects while strengthening my skills in collaboration and leadership.

Chicago is home, and the city's energy is in everything I do. Outside of work you'll find me behind the DJ decks, testing new material at open mics as an amateur stand-up comic, or locked in on Marvel Rivals, where I've reached champion with Daredevil.

It might look like a lot of different hobbies, but they all come down to the same thing I love about engineering: timing, reading the room, and putting in the reps until it clicks. Now I'm looking for my next opportunity as a full stack engineer, where I can ship meaningful products and make an impact on a team.
